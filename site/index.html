How it works

Pick the services you depend on during a quick first-launch setup. Downbar polls each one's official public status feed — Statuspage, Instatus, AWS, Apple, Google Cloud, Azure, or a plain website check — and folds every result into one tidy menu and a single menu-bar color.

When something breaks, you get a notification you can click straight through to the incident. When it's fixed, you get the all-clear. Reorder, mute, or set a severity threshold so you only hear about what's worth hearing about.
